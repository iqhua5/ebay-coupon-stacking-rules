## Where working eBay coupons actually come from

The coupons that reliably work are the ones eBay itself issues. Everything else on aggregator sites is a mix of expired codes, category-specific offers reposted out of context, and codes targeted to other people's accounts.

Four sources, in the order you should check them:

1. **eBay's Deals page.** This is where eBay publishes its current site-issued codes, usually percentage-off offers for select categories like fashion, tech or toys. Each code links to its terms — eligible items, minimum spend, end date, per-account limits. If a code isn't on this page or in your account, treat it as suspect.
2. **Your own eBay account and inbox.** eBay sends targeted coupons to specific accounts, and personal coupons show up under My eBay > Account. A code that worked for someone else's screenshot may simply not exist on your account — that's usually why a code says "not valid" even though it hasn't expired.
3. **Your cashback portal's eBay page.** TopCashback lists eBay coupons on its eBay page, and those are the ones you can use without voiding your cash back (more on this below).
4. **Seller-side price cuts.** Multi-item discounts, seller-set markdowns and Best Offer are price adjustments, not codes. They generally don't interfere with cash back the way codes can.
